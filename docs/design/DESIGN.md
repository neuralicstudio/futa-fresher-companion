# Sabi design system

Source of truth for values: [tokens.css](tokens.css) and [tokens.json](tokens.json), generated by [tools/tokens.mjs](tools/tokens.mjs). This file describes how they are used. Dark values are in the token files; every role keeps its hue in both modes. The band colours are the same in both modes.

## Overview

The gold line. Sabi draws a line from "I am lost" to "I found it": the route you walk, the gold trim on a gown, the thread from a fact to its source. Committed in the shell and the band, calm in the content. Mobile first, light by default (students read it outdoors), a designed dark mode, and a Lite mode for weak data. Voice: a senior student who knows every shortcut. Short, warm, exact.

## Colors

### The band and the shell
Night maroon `#31060b` owns the top bar and the top of every screen's band. The band's lower edge follows the time of day: dawn `band-dawn` (warm, toward the gold), afternoon `band-day` (the shell maroon), evening `band-dusk`, night `band-night`. Text on the band is `on-band`, secondary `on-band-muted`; both pass 4.5:1 on every edge.

### The line
Gold `#f3be55` on maroon. On light paper the line and icon details use `accent` `#9d6300` (3:1 or more, it is never text there). In dark mode `accent` is the gold.

### Maroon
`maroon` `#6e1924` is the primary action and the University chip. As text it is the `link` role (maroon in light, gold in dark).

### Neutrals and washes
Paper, well and raised, tinted toward maroon. `maroon-wash` holds the active tab capsule, codes and initials; `gold-wash` holds "Today", time chips and the department's Timetable card.

### Named rules
- **Gold means current, confirmed or the way.** The line, the current tab's detail, Today, Verified, the next class.
- **State colours carry words and icons,** never colour alone.

## Typography

One family, the phone's `system-ui`: zero font bytes. A 1.2 scale, five weights with one role each: body 400, labels 500, titles 650, headings 800, and 900 for the band titles, the ticket's time and the wordmark. Times and counts use tabular numerals. Smallest text 14px.

## Layout

A single content column, 720px at most, 16px gutters. Each screen opens with its band (title, lead, back link on detail screens); content follows on paper. Home: the ticket overlaps the band, then the line of stations; from 768px the stations and the saved box sit side by side. Forms sit on a raised card that overlaps the band.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth comes from the band (a gradient sky over paper), the paper ladder, and one lifted object: the ticket (`shadow-lift`, as a drop-shadow so its notches cast correctly). Dialogs use `shadow-float`. No glows.

## Shapes

Controls 10px, plates 14px, cards 18px; pills only for circles and chips. The ticket has two notches and a tear line (one print artifact, on one element). Station rings are circles.

## Motion

Calm. Feedback 120ms; state 220ms; page transitions and the stamp 420ms; one authored entrance per screen 760ms (the line drawing in), once per session, only on screen. Ease-out to arrive, ease-in to leave at 65%, no bounce. Under reduced motion or Lite, nothing moves; state still changes.

## Components

### The mark
An S drawn as a route between two stations. Gold on night maroon. 28px in the top bar, 96px on first sync and the index. App icons in `brand/`.

### Icons
24px grid, 2px stroke, round ends. Each icon has an ink body and one `.ac` detail that turns gold (accent) when current. One set, drawn in house.

### The line and stations
A 4px line with 22px rings. A station is a 44px+ link: ring, title, one line of what is there, an optional gold chip, an arrow. Done stations fill; waiting stations are dashed.

### The ticket
Next class: label, time at 2.488rem/900 with "in N min", course, and a stub with the venue and "Full timetable". Without a department it asks the student to choose one.

### The band
Night-maroon sky with contour lines at 20% gold. Optional photo slot behind it (greyscale, 50%, under a band scrim). Titles in `on-band`.

### Buttons
Primary (maroon), gold (on the band), secondary (2px edge), quiet (underlined), danger. 48px, eight states each.

### The source seal
Verified (gold, check), Unverified (dashed), May be outdated (amber, warning), the date, a Source disclosure. It stamps on when an admin marks a record verified.

### States
Loading: skeleton (loops only on screen). Empty, error, offline, no match: a line piece and words. Offline also breaks the top bar's line.

### Lite
A switch with a hint and a reason line. A "Lite" chip in the top bar while on.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:
- Let the line carry state: offline, progress, now.
- Put the seal beside every fact that came from someone.
- Keep every target 44px or larger and every focus ring visible, on paper and on the band.

### Don't:
- No eyebrows, tracked capitals, middle-dot strings, emoji icons, glows, purple, gradient text or cards in cards.
- No pictures of people; no drawn FUTA buildings or real geography; contour lines are decoration.
- No maroon text in dark mode; no gold text on light paper (use `accent`, at 3:1, for non-text only).
- No invented facts. Sample data says "(sample)".
