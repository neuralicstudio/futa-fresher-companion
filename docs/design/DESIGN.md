# Sabi design system (round 3)

The values are in [tokens.css](tokens.css) and [tokens.json](tokens.json), generated by [tools/tokens.mjs](tools/tokens.mjs); this file says how they are used. The hex values above are the light-mode roles. Dark values are in the token files.

## Overview

**The flyer.** Every screen leads with a layered hero: a FUTA photo (from the visual pack) or a drawn scene, a scrim tinted by the time of day, and the title set big. One sticker holds the number that matters on that screen. Below the hero is a calm working body on warm paper, with glass where something floats over a picture.

The mode is Operate: a student doing a task. The expression lives in the hero, the stickers, the drawings and the type, never in the controls, which are standard.

Voice: a senior student who knows every shortcut. Short, warm, exact.

## Colors

- **Night `#290509`** is the brand surface in both modes. It is the hero scrim, the tab bar, stickers, the go card and the first run.
- **Maroon** is the primary action and the University scope.
- **Gold** means current, confirmed or the way. It marks the active tab, the Verified seal, stickers, the "you are here" dot and figures on night.
- **Accent `#9d6300`** is gold for icon details on paper, at 3:1 or more. It is never used for text.
- **Jade, sky and coral** are the aso-ebi colours: notice scopes (School, Department, SUG), chart series, door tiles and the drawn people's clothes. Each has a wash for chips that carry ink text.
- **Time of day:**
  - The scrim takes `tint-morning` (warm), `tint-afternoon` (maroon), `tint-evening` (coral maroon) or `tint-night` (blue-black).
  - The drawn sky takes the matching `sky-<daypart>-1/2` pair.
  - Every text pair over every tint passes AA, measured over a white pixel.
- **The scene palette** (sun, moon, hills, rock, leaf, trunk, earth, wall, roof, pane, three skin tones, hair, cloth) belongs to the drawings only. It is mode-independent.

## Typography

- **Two faces, both OFL, subset, 23 KB:**
  - **Bricolage Grotesque 800** for display: hero titles, door titles, the next-class time, sticker figures, card display titles, section heads.
  - **Atkinson Hyperlegible Next 400 and 700** for everything else.
- **Weights:** three in use (400, 700, 800). `font-display: swap` with a system fallback.
- **Scale:** ratio 1.2. 14px is the smallest text. Hero titles are 2.488rem, rising to 2.986rem from 768px and on Welcome and You're set.
- **Numbers:** times and counts use tabular figures.

## Layout

- **Hero first,** full-bleed:
  - Home: 620px
  - detail screens: 380px
  - Settings and the sheet: 300px
  - Welcome and first sync: the full screen, with the scene in the top 60% and the text on night below.
- **Content column:** 720px at most (1040px for grids), with 16px gutters.
- **Cards that lift onto the hero:** Home's next-class card, the department facts and the log in and register forms overlap the hero foot by 96 to 132px.
- **From 768px:** doors go to four across, Home's lower cards to two, building and school cards to two columns.
- **From 1024px:** the tab bar moves into the top bar.

## Elevation and depth

There are four layers:
1. The picture.
2. The scrim.
3. Glass (paper-3 at 84%, blur 18px, a hairline light edge, `shadow-2`).
4. Solid cards on paper (`shadow-1`).

The gold sticker and the active tab carry `shadow-gold`, which has an offset; it is not a halo. In dark mode, shadows become hairline edges. In Lite, glass is solid.

## Shapes

- **Pills:** controls, chips, filters, the tab bar, stickers and rings.
- **Cards and photos:** 24px. Fields: 14px. Sheets and dialogs: 32px.

## Motion

- **Durations:** feedback 120ms; state 220ms; page transitions, rings filling and the stamp 480ms; one entrance per screen 900ms.
- **Ambient loops** (48s cloud drift, stars, the map route, the ring sheen) run only on screen, never in Lite, never under reduced motion.
- **Easing:** ease-out for arrivals. `ease-pop` (one small overshoot) is kept for the stamp only.

## Components

- **The mark:** an S drawn as a route from a station to a rising sun. Gold on night.
- **Icons:** 24px grid, 2px stroke, an ink body and one gold `.ac` detail.
- **Hero:** a photo slot over a scene, the scrim under the text only, and an optional back pill, greeting chip, code chip and sticker.
- **Sticker:** 88px (64px small). A night or gold disc, a ring with a 6px stroke, a display figure and a unit.
- **Next-class card:** glass, a sticker counting down to the class, the time at 2.488rem, the course and venue, and "Full timetable".
- **Doors:** four coloured tiles (maroon, jade wash, gold, sky wash), each with a disc and icon, a display title, one line of what's there, and an optional chip.
- **Photo cards:** 4:3 or square. A name on the scrim only when a sign in the photo shows it. A credit chip on large photos.
- **Charts:** stacked bar, horizontal bars and a donut. Every value is also written as text. No chart over five series.
- **Seal:** Verified (gold), Unverified (dashed), May be outdated (amber). The date and a Source disclosure.
- **State scenes:** a student in a tinted circle with one icon: waiting, offline, detour, done, searching.
- **Visual pack card:** a mosaic preview, a status chip, the size, a progress sticker while downloading, an action, and the "Only on Wi-Fi" switch.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:
- Lead each screen with a picture: a photo when the pack is on the phone, a drawing when it isn't.
- Put the scrim under every line of text that sits on a picture.
- Keep one sticker per screen, holding a real number.
- Name a building only when its sign shows in the photo.
- Keep 44px targets and visible focus on paper, glass and night.

### Don't:
- No eyebrows, tracked capitals, middle-dot strings, emoji icons, gradient text, purple, or cards in cards.
- No real person's likeness. People are the four drawn students; lecturers get initials.
- No drawn real FUTA buildings or real geography. Scenes and the map are generic and say so.
- No photos on first load, none in Lite, and nothing over 60 KB.
- No invented facts. Sample data says "(sample)".
