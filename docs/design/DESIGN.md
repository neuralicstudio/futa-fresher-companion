# Sabi design system

Source of truth for values: [tokens.css](tokens.css) and [tokens.json](tokens.json), generated by [tools/tokens.mjs](tools/tokens.mjs). This file describes how they are used. Dark values are in the token files; every role keeps its hue in both modes.

## Overview

A friendly, confident guide, not a corporate dashboard. The world is academic regalia (a maroon gown with a gold hood trim) carrying the brief's wayfinding idea: Home is a signpost at a junction, and every fact carries a seal saying how sure we are, where it came from and when. Mobile first, light by default because students read it outdoors; dark mode is a designed second palette.

## Colors

### Primary
Maroon `#6e1924` owns the shell (the top bar) and the primary action. It is never used as body text in dark mode (it falls to 1.5:1 on the dark page); the `link` role switches to gold there.

### Secondary
Gold `#f3be55` is the trim: the 4px edge under the top bar, the current tab, "Today" in the timetable, the verified seal, the pressed cell in a choice. Text on gold is always ink. Gold is text only on the dark page or on maroon, never on light paper (except `gold-ink` at large sizes).

### Neutral
Paper `#fbf6f5`, a well `#f3eae9` for the bottom tabs and plates, raised `#fffcfc` for fields. All neutrals are tinted toward the maroon hue (25), never cream (Hallmark gate 65, Impeccable `cream-palette`).

### Named rules
- **Gold means current or confirmed.** Never decoration.
- **State colours sit on top, with words and icons.** Error, warning ("may be outdated") and success always carry text and an icon, never colour alone.

## Typography

One family: the phone's own `system-ui`, so the app loads zero font bytes. Hierarchy is a fixed rem scale at a 1.2 ratio and four weights, one role each: body 400, labels 500, titles 650, display 800. Times, course codes and counts use tabular numerals. The smallest text is 14px (meta, hints, badges); body is 16px at 1.55.

### Hierarchy
Screen title 1.728rem/800 · section 1.44rem/800 · item title 1.2rem/650 · body 1rem/400 · meta 0.875rem/400. The Home greeting may use 2.074rem.

## Layout

A single content column, 720px wide at most, 16px gutters. Rows are separated by hairlines rather than boxed as cards. Space above a heading is larger than below it. From 768px the bottom tabs move into the top bar and Home goes two-column (signs left, saved status and the latest notice right).

## Elevation & Depth

Flat. Depth comes from the paper ladder (paper, well, raised). One shadow, `shadow-float`, only for things that float (dialogs); in dark mode it becomes an inset hairline.

## Shapes

6px corners on controls and plates, 10px on dialogs, pills only for seals and the Today tag. The sign is the one bespoke shape: a plate with a pointed right end.

## Components

### Buttons
Primary (maroon fill), secondary (2px edge), quiet (underlined link-style), danger (error edge). 48px tall, 8 states: default, hover, focus, active, disabled (opacity 0.55, not-allowed, native attribute), loading (spinner replaces nothing, label stays), error and success shown next to the button, not on it.

### Chips / choice
A segmented choice of square cells sharing edges; the chosen cell is gold.

### Cards / containers
No cards for list items. Plates (raised or well) hold one thing: the saved-data box, a verify item, an empty or error state.

### Inputs / fields
Label above, hint below in a reserved line, 1px muted edge (3:1), the focus ring inset, the border never changes width. Errors: red edge, icon, text tied by `aria-describedby`.

### Navigation
Maroon top bar with the gold trim; four bottom tabs on phones (Home, Map, Academics, Notices), the current one gold-trimmed and maroon (gold in dark). Tabs scroll sideways at very large text instead of overlapping.

### Signature: the signpost and the source seal
The signpost: maroon signs on a maroon pole capped in gold, each a destination and one line of what is there, with an arrow. The source seal: a status pill (Verified gold with a check, Unverified dashed with an open circle, May be outdated amber with a warning), the updated date, and a Source disclosure.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:
- Put the seal beside every fact that came from someone.
- Say what is empty and what to do next, in the app's own words.
- Keep every target 44px or larger and every focus ring visible.

### Don't:
- No eyebrows, tracked capitals, middle-dot strings, emoji icons, gradients, glows or cards in cards.
- No maroon text in dark mode; no gold text on light paper.
- No invented campus facts, names, numbers or map geography. Sample data says "(sample)".
