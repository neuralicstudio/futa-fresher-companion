# Notice: design mockups only, not for reuse or shipping

These twelve images are the approved Sabi screen mockups. They are here so the frontend team can **look at the intended design** after cloning the repo. They are pictures of a design, not assets.

## Read this before you use anything here

- **They contain AI-generated people.** Faces, bodies and clothing on screens such as Register and Login were generated by an AI tool. They are not real students, and none of them is cleared for use.
- **They contain sample data.** Every name, course, lecturer, grade, date, time, number, notice and place detail is made up for the picture. None of it is real FUTA data. Do not copy it into the app, the database or any document.
- **They contain photographs that are not licensed for reuse.** Campus photos fill the backgrounds and thumbnails. The night photo of the FUTA gate comes from FUTA's official Facebook page, and **FUTA's written permission to use it is still pending**. The other photos have no recorded licence. Nothing in these images may be cropped, extracted, re-published or shipped.
- **Their credits are not real.** Any photo credit or licence text drawn inside a mockup was invented by the picture's author. The real credits, read from the real file pages, are in [`../CREDITS.md`](../CREDITS.md).
- **They are not the final design.** Where the mockups and the written design differ, the differences are fixes on purpose. See [`../README.md`](../README.md) and [`../SCREENS.md`](../SCREENS.md). Build from the tokens, components, copy and screen specs, not by tracing these pictures.

**Do not** put these images, or any part of them, in the app, a store listing, marketing, slides, social media or any other document. If you are FUTA or another rights holder and want one removed, open an issue on this repository.
